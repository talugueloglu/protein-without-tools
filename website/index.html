Abstract

Frontier large language models increasingly incorporate raw biological sequences alongside scientific text during pretraining, raising a fundamental question: do general-purpose reasoning models acquire genuine representations of protein folding thermodynamics and molecular recognition, or do they primarily rely on sequence-to-literature recall and 1D amino-acid heuristics?

We introduce ProteinBench, a reproducible eight-task evaluation suite spanning forward folding (N = 100), inverse folding on stripped UNK backbones (N = 100), deep twilight-zone remote homology detection down to <15% sequence identity (N = 120), 2026 CAFA experimental function prediction (N = 60), monomer folding alanine scan ranking (N = 52), monomer-stability-controlled interface binding alanine scan ranking (N = 38), de novo fold and crystal-motif scaffolding (N = 40), and 16-round closed-loop epitope binder design with an AlphaFold2 oracle (N = 12 × 16 turns).

By evaluating models across both No-Code (pure parametric weights and chain-of-thought) and With-Code (air-gapped Python + NumPy/SciPy) tracks—and stratifying targets by exact 3D structural novelty (Foldseek TM-score) and pretraining memorization exposure—ProteinBench cleanly separates arithmetic coordinate bottlenecks, literature de-anonymization, and true biophysical generalization against nine specialist structural biology baselines.

Pillar 1 • Anti-Memorization

Sequence De-Anonymization vs. True Novelty

Stripping PDB IDs is not enough—LLMs recognize famous sequences like Keytruda (5B8C) from memory. ProteinBench explicitly contrasts Known Pre-2026 PDBs against May–Nov 2026 experimental releases (9YOY TM=0.28, KRAS×LZTR1, 9S83) and unpublished synthetic antibodies (PP489×TIGIT).

Pillar 2 • Biophysical Controls

Same-AA Decoys & Pure Handshake Filtering

Static 1D lookup tables (Kyte–Doolittle, Bogan–Thorn Trp/Tyr/Arg) inflate raw correlations. Our balanced 18-site ranking panels pair every hotspot with a same-amino-acid neutral decoy (forcing 1D tables to a 50.0% coin flip) and filter out monomer-melting mutations via paired abundance scans.

Pillar 3 • Tool Disentanglement

No-Code Weights vs. Air-Gapped NumPy

Giving an LLM numpy/scipy with zero network or bio-libraries lets it compute exact 3D virtual-Cβ burial and Ramachandran angles in Tasks 2, 7, 8—while proving whether code alone can or cannot rescue pure 1D sequence physics in Tasks 1, 3, 5, 6.
