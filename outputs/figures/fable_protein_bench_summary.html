6 Subtle Protein Benchmark Pitfalls We Caught & Engineered Out

Why naive LLM biology benchmarks overestimate reasoning—and how Fable-Protein-Bench closes every loophole.

Pitfall / Shortcut What Goes Wrong Without Guardrails Our Implemented Fix
1. Foldseek 3diaa E-value vs. True 3D TM-score 47WY (14.8% SeqID) gets weak Foldseek E=6.50, yet its true 3D TM-score to pre-cutoff PDB is 0.84 (1.55Å RMSD). Computed exact Foldseek mode="tmalign" + Kabsch SO(3) TM-score across all 100 Stage 1 targets; mined 18 genuinely novel folds (mean PDB TM = 0.431).
2. Post-Cutoff Re-Crystallization Leaks RCSB top-35 pagination hid pre-2026 structures of ubiquitous proteins like Lysozyme (38BH) & KRAS (11GH). Combined MMseqs2 (rows=100, return_explain_metadata=True) + Foldseek pdb100 pre-cutoff SeqID; purged all leaked re-crystallizations.
3. Static 1D Hydrophobicity Cheat on Alanine Scans Predicting Trp/Phe/Leu → Ala is always destabilizing yields Raw Spearman ρ = 0.339 with zero 3D understanding! Added AA-Demeaned Context Correlation (AA-Ctx ρ), which scores 0.000 on 1D tables and isolates true buried-core vs. surface-exposed context.
4. Generic "Binding" Spam in CAFA GO Prediction Guessing high-prior root neighbors ties BLAST on unweighted F_max (0.322 vs 0.323). Primary metric is Information-Content-weighted wF_max (bits), where homology beats Naive 0.357 vs 0.127 on Molecular Function.
5. EAAAK Coiled-Coil Rods in De Novo Design Periodic AEAAAKEAAAK... helices trick ESMFold into pLDDT > 90 despite being non-globular infinite rods. 5-Gate filter enforces Shannon entropy ≥ 3.2 bits, 3-mer repeat cap, globular Rg ≤ 2.35·L^0.38, MPNN scRMSD ≤ 2Å, and <30% SeqID.
6. Unbindable Dimers & Anthropic Eval Overlap in Binders Obligate enzyme dimers may be unbindable by miniproteins; TREM2/15-PGDH/RBX1 were used in Anthropic's Aug-2026 binder study. Curated 12 proven-bindable epitopes (4 RFD4/ProtDBench + 5 June-2026 BoltzProt-1 novel + 3 July–Aug 2026 wet-lab de novo minibinder crystals), excluding Anthropic's internal eval set.
